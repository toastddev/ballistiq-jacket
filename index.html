Company Heritage

Decades of material expertise, now in service of protection.

From industrial glass manufacturing to advanced ballistic systems — a single thread of precision runs through it all.

Ballistiq Defense Private Limited is founded on the manufacturing foundation of Chandra Lakshmi Glass — an industrial safety and toughened glass manufacturer with over 30 years of precision material processing experience.

That heritage informs everything: our understanding of material behavior under stress, our quality discipline, our engineering culture, and our commitment to products that perform when it matters.

Ballistiq Defense represents the next chapter: applying that deep material knowledge to the design and manufacture of ballistic protection systems for modern operational requirements.
